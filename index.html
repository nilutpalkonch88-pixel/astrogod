Locate Disease Astrologically

V. P. Goel · divisional-chart medicine

Core method: Lagna + 6th (roga) + 8th (chronic) + most-afflicted planet, confirmed when the SAME planet/house repeats in D-6 & D-60. Shashtiamsha (D-60) = chronic imprint · D-6 = acute illness · D-8 = sudden shocks.

Surya→heart/bones · Chandra→mind/fluids · Mangal→blood/inflammation · Budha→nerves/skin · Guru→liver/fat · Shukra→kidney/reproductive · Shani→joints/chronic vata · Rahu→undiagnosed/toxins · Ketu→fevers/scars. Remedy follows the CAUSE planet. ⚕️ Study lens — doctors diagnose.

Yogini Dasha

36-year prediction cycle

Core method: 8 Yoginis × fixed years (Mangala 1 · Pingala 2 · Dhanya 3 · Bhramari 4 · Bhadrika 5 · Ulka 6 · Siddha 7 · Sankata 8 = 36), repeating from birth-nakshatra order. Dhanya/Bhadrika/Siddha uplift; Ulka/Sankata mature.

Dhanya–Guru: wealth, marriage, children. Siddha–Shukra: luxury, vehicles, love. Bhramari–Mangal: land & courage. Ulka–Shani: delays & tests. Sankata–Rahu: sudden turns — stay humble, no speculation. Your live timeline is computed in section 04.
📜

Laghu Parashari

Functional yogas in verse form

Core method: kendra+trikona lords = functional benefics; 3/6/11 lords = functional malefics. Kendra lord + trikona lord joined = Raja Yoga. 2nd + 5th/9th/11th = Dhana yoga. Dusthana malefic in dusthana = Vipareeta Raja.

A yoga-planet's dasha delivers BOTH houses it owns. 8th lord spoils; 2nd/12th neutral. Your chart's kendra/trikona/dusthana yogas are auto-detected in section 03 (My Life Prediction).
📚

Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra

The Parashara foundation

Core method: graha (what) + bhava (where) + dasha (when) + gochara (trigger). 12 bhavas from self→moksha; 9 grahas from soul (Surya) to moksha (Ketu). Promise without dasha sleeps.

1 self · 2 wealth · 3 courage · 4 home · 5 progeny/mantra · 6 disease/enemies · 7 spouse · 8 longevity · 9 fortune · 10 career · 11 gains · 12 foreign/moksha. Karakas: Guru=progeny, Shukra=spouse, Shani=sorrow/service, Rahu=foreign hunger.
🔥

Phaladeepika

Mantreshwara · lamp of results

Core method: judge each bhava by (a) its lord's strength (b) occupants (c) aspects. Malefics in 3/6/11 grow with time; benefics in kendra protect the whole chart like lamps in a hall.

10th strong = rank · 7th pure + Shukra = devotion · 5th + Guru = brilliant progeny · 4th = property bliss · 9th = fortune. Weak bhava lord in dusthana = that area needs remedy + effort in its dasha.
